An amp that happens to have a screen

NAM Player today is a tone browser that grew a rig editor, then had its stage half amputated. Three of four nav slots go to shopping; none to playing. These five screens put playing back at the centre.

A footswitch cannot change your sound without changing your screen. applyStackToEngine() returns early unless the rig editor is the visible screen — so a stomp has to open that screen for any sound to happen.

Navigation isn’t a side effect of the stomp. It is the mechanism. Underneath it: there is no “live rig” in this application — the live rig is a property of a screen being visible.
